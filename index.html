About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Pan-Atlantic University in Lagos, and the security side of computing is where I'm most at home: how systems get set up, how they're kept safe, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Currently working on

Detecting Scam Messages and Phishing Links in Nigeria Using Machine Learning: Robustness Against AI-Reworded Attacks

My final year project. Social engineering cost Nigerians about ₦8 billion in 2023, much of it through SMS and WhatsApp messages: fake credit alerts, “update your BVN” links and fake loan offers. I'm building a dataset of Nigerian scam messages and phishing links in English and Pidgin, training models to detect them, and testing whether they still work when a scam is reworded by tools like ChatGPT. The best model will become a web app where anyone can paste a message or link and get a verdict.
